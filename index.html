University of Layyah-A Growing Hub

The University of Layyah (UL) has rapidly emerged as a vital engine for academic excellence and regional development in South Punjab. As the first full-fledged independent public sector university in the Layyah District, it is successfully bridging the higher education gap for local youth by providing accessible, modern learning opportunities.
Milestone Achievements & Strategic Governance:
Under the leadership of Vice Chancellor Prof. Dr. Muhammad Zubair Abu Bakar, the university is undergoing swift organizational and infrastructural growth.
• Strengthened Leadership: In 2026, the university welcomed major statutory officers—including a permanent Registrar, Treasurer, and Controller of Examinations—instilling a new era of administrative transparency and academic governance.
• Active Syndicate Oversight: High-level educational planning is actively moving forward. The 9th Syndicate Meeting of the University of Layyah, chaired by the Punjab Higher Education Minister, Rana Sikandar Hayat, convened in mid-October 2026 to review financial, research, and infrastructure priorities.
• Capacity Building: Collaborations with the National Academy of Higher Education (NAHE) under the HEC umbrella have led to major faculty and management development training workshops to uplift teaching standards.
